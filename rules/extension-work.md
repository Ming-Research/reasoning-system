# Extension: reviewing the work against the decisions

For the review of a work whose code, specifications or configuration the decisions govern, once it is written.

## 1. What to read

- The whole change, and what it directly affects.
- The decisions on the questions these meet, those the work adds or revises among them, with what each rejected and
  replaced. Finding them is your own reading.

## 2. What to check

For each change in the work:

1. **Each real choice it makes is governed by a decision**: name the decision. A choice none governs is a finding, a
   missing addition.
2. **It contradicts no decision**, and carries out no option a decision rejected, without a revision in the work.
3. **What it deletes**: name the decisions it touched. A decision left with nothing carrying it out, or a choice
   rejected or replaced still carried out, is a finding.
4. **What the decisions commit to**: for each decision the work carries out, adds or revises, name what carries out
   each part. A part missing, half done or a placeholder is a finding.

## 3. How to report

Each check says what its judgment rests on: passed, a finding, or cannot be verified; no evidence is no pass. A finding
quotes the work and the decision it fails against, and says what would resolve it.

## 4. Reviewing again

The review runs once over the whole work. The review again is given the findings, the answer to each and what changed
since. It reviews only the fixes that rewrote logic or behaviour beyond a local repair, and what each touched; it checks
that every other fix, listed by its author, is not of that kind; and it raises nothing outside these.
