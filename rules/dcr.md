# Reasoning rules: the design coherence review (DCR)

For the review that checks a change to the records. Read `reading.md` and `writing.md` first.

## 1. What to read

- The change to the records, whole.
- Every record it adds, changes or removes, with what each rests on.
- Every record resting on those, found through `Rests on`.
- What the format check printed.

Say which records you read.

## 2. What to check

1. **The records cohere.** Every premise exists, and the derivation that cites it uses it. There is no cycle. No
   derivation contradicts another, or narrows one without saying so.
2. **What rests on a change was examined again.** Every record resting on what changed holds still or was revised,
   and the log says which.
3. **The facts cited are in the records.** Whether a fact holds is the project's, not this review's.
4. **The reasons are derivations.** Each reason derives from its cited premises and says its kind of ground. None is
   someone's words: the owner's input enters only as the constitution or as a ruling on a card.
5. **Each card says what its revision says,** in other words but with the same content, and every revised derivation
   has its card.
6. **The records keep their format,** as the check reports it.

## 3. How to report

For each finding, quote both sides and the premises each rests on, and say what would resolve it. A finding is never
resolved by citing someone's words.
