# Extension: cards

An extension of the reasoning system, not part of it: how a revision is put to the owner and ruled. Read `writing.md`
first.

## 1. A card

A card is a revision put to the owner. It stands in the project's design where the design reaches its question, so
that the text before it gives the background, where the design stands and why this is asked; the text around it does
not repeat it. It has:

- one question saying what it decides, in plain words, with no internal names;
- at least two workable options, each with what it does, its cost and its risks; never one approach against not
  doing it, so that whether to do something becomes how much of it to do; no option made up to fill a count;
- the options considered and found not workable, each with why;
- the recommended option;
- its confidence from 1 to 5, why, and what would overturn it; and where it departs from an earlier ruling;
- the derivations it adds or changes.

## 2. Keeping cards

- The card and its revision say the same thing in different words; the review of the revision checks that they do.
- The review checks what the card and the derivations it cites show: each option workable, each dismissal a reason
  that holds, the obvious ways covered. A workable option it happens to see missing is a finding; it has no duty to
  search for more.
- A ruled card is never changed: a question that changes comes back under the same key, saying what changed; a question
  no longer asked is withdrawn, saying why.
