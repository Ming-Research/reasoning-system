# Extension: cards

For agents that put an addition or a revision to the owner as a card, and for its review.

## 1. A card

A card stands in the work's design, where the design reaches its question, so that the text before it gives the
background, where the work stands and why this is asked; the text around it does not repeat it. It is written in the
owner's language and in plain words, each term of the project explained where it is first used, and has:

- its question;
- for a revision, the choice it replaces and the base that changed;
- every option, each with what it does, what it gives and costs against the others, what else changes with it, and why
  it wins or loses on the premises; the one recommended;
- the approaches explored and beaten, each with what beat it;
- its confidence from 1 to 5, 5 when known facts settle it and 1 when it rests on judgment, why, and what would
  overturn it.

A decision the revision changes whose new choice is real is a question of its own on the same card, with the same parts.

## 2. Ruling

- The owner takes, on each question, the option recommended, or another approach with a sentence of why.
- A ruled card is never changed. Until what it decides is recorded, a question that changes comes back as a new card
  saying what changed, and one no longer asked is withdrawn, saying why; once recorded, a change is a revision.
