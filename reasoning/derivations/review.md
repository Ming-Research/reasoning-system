Decision dcr-scope: The design coherence review checks the records and their revisions only, instead of also the project's work or how a revision reached the owner, because coherence is what the system answers for, and those are its extensions.

Rests on: C:coherence, C:extension

Decision dcr-checks: The review checks that every premise a derivation lists is used and none contradicts another, that what rested on a change was examined again, that every fact cited is in the records, that every reason is a derivation, and that the records pass the check, because these keep the records coherent through a revision, and whether a premise is used or two derivations contradict is judged by reading, not by the check.

Rests on: C:coherence, C:facts-given, C:owner-inputs, D:dependents-examined, D:words-not-reasons, D:check-script
