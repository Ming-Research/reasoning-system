Decision follow-derivations: Work the derivations govern does what they decide, keeps a rejected approach rejected unless what changed is said, and raises a departure instead of departing alone, because keeping the work in line with the decisions is the extension, and changing a decision is the owner's.

Rests on: C:extension, C:owner-inputs

Decision derive-before-compare: A problem is solved from the records first, the governing derivations followed up to the articles and the solution derived from them and the problem, and approaches are compared only where the records leave a trade-off or a revision would be better, instead of several approaches compared first with the records as a reference and revised after the choice, because every decision follows from the axioms and the decisions before it, and a choice made before its derivation follows from nothing.

Rests on: C:extension, C:coherence, D:trade-off-to-owner

Rejected:
- Several approaches compared first, the records read as a reference and revised once one is chosen: rejected because the choice then comes before its derivation, and approaches not derived from the records are often not workable at all (C:coherence).

Decision challenge-the-chain: A solution derived from the records names the derivations that constrain it most and says, for each, whether revising it would give a clearly better solution by the same articles, going no higher than the articles, instead of keeping within the derivations as they stand, because a better solution that only a revision gives is found only by looking for it, and the articles are the owner's.

Rests on: C:extension, C:owner-inputs, F:decisions-anchor, D:revise-not-work-around

Rejected:
- Keeping each solution within the derivations as they stand: rejected because what the existing decisions decide narrows what is considered, so that a better solution behind a revision is not found (F:decisions-anchor).

Decision work-review: The project's work is checked against the derivations by a review of its own: a choice no derivation governs, work contradicting a derivation or implementing a rejected approach, a derivation left with nothing implementing it, a commitment left unfinished, and a solution whose chain was not challenged, with the governing derivations found by the reviewer's own reading, because the work must do what the decisions decide and decide nothing they do not.

Rests on: C:extension, C:membership, D:follow-derivations, D:challenge-the-chain

Rejected:
- A mapping in the records from each derivation to the work it governs: rejected because it serves no coherence of the records, and a reviewer who reads the work finds what governs it (C:membership).
