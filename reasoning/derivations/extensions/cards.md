Decision card-content: A revision is put to the owner as a card asking one question in plain words, with at least two workable options, each with what it does, its cost and its risks, never one approach against not doing it, and the options considered and found not workable, each with why, the recommended option, its confidence from 1 to 5 with why and what would overturn it, and the derivations it adds or changes; the review checks what the card and the derivations it cites show, that each option is workable, each dismissal has a reason that holds and the obvious ways are covered, and takes a workable option it happens to see missing as a finding, with no duty to search for more; because the owner rules by trading workable approaches off, which a choice between doing and not doing leaves nothing to trade, and weighs a recommendation by how firmly it rests, while a set number of workable options above what the question has is filled with ones nobody would pick, and an option found not workable, kept with why, is not explored again.

Rests on: C:extension, C:owner-inputs, D:trade-off-to-owner, F:padded-options, F:paths-explored-again

Rejected:
- At least three workable options: rejected because a question with two workable answers then gets a third made up to fill the count (F:padded-options).

Decision card-in-design: A card stands in the project's design where the design reaches its question, the text before it giving the background, where the design stands and why it is asked, instead of standing alone, because the owner rules a card by its context.

Rests on: C:extension, F:owner-rules-in-context

Rejected:
- A card standing alone, with its background written into it: rejected because the owner does not understand a card apart from the design it decides (F:owner-rules-in-context).

Decision card-equals-revision: A card and its revision say the same thing in other words, and the review of the revision checks that they do, because what the owner rules must be what enters the records.

Rests on: C:extension, C:owner-inputs, C:coherence

Decision ruled-card-fixed: A ruled card is never changed: a question that changes comes back under the same key, saying what changed, and a question no longer asked is withdrawn, saying why, because what the owner ruled must stay as ruled, as the log entry carrying it out names it.

Rests on: C:extension, D:log
